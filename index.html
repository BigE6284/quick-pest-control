The pests we get called about most

Every house is different. Tell us what you've seen and where, and we'll tell you what we can do about it.

Talk it through on a call

Ants and crawling insects

Trails along counters, baseboards or window sills usually mean a nest nearby. We'll help you find the source, not just the trail.

Mice and rodents

Droppings, scratching in the walls at night, or chewed packaging. Rodents get in through gaps you'd never notice — we'll look at where they're coming in, not just where they've been.

Spiders and other insects

Webs in corners, basements and garages, or insects you can't identify. Describe what you've seen and we'll tell you whether it's something we handle.

Prevention and entry points

Already dealt with a problem and don't want it back? Ask about sealing gaps, common entry points, and practical steps for your home.

Something else? If your pest isn't listed here, call and ask — if it's not something we handle, we'll tell you straight.

Questions before you call?
